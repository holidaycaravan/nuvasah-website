About Nuvasah

Nuvasah Interiors was founded in 2023 carrying forward a vision that good interior design shouldn't be reserved for sprawling luxury homes—it should make sense where most of us actually live. We founded Nuvasah on a simple idea: bringing thoughtful, functional design within reach of everyday spaces.

We work with homeowners across Delhi NCR, mostly first-time renovators and families upgrading a space they've lived in for years. We keep our process personal and our team small on purpose — every project gets real attention, not a template pulled off a shelf.

Good Design Yearns for Simplicity

We believe a home should feel calm before it feels impressive. That means clean lines over clutter, materials that age well over materials that just look good on day one, and layouts that work for real mornings and real evenings — not just photographs.

Minimalism, to us, isn't about having less. It's about making sure everything in a room has a reason to be there. A mid-century-inspired warmth, Scandinavian restraint, and an eye for how Indian homes are actually lived in — that's the balance we aim for in every project.

Function first, always. A beautiful room that doesn't work for your routine isn't a finished room.

Warmth over perfection. We'd rather a space feel lived-in and honest than staged.

Budget-conscious by design. Thoughtful choices, not just expensive ones, are what make a home feel put-together.

We're also happy to work within Vastu considerations where it matters to your family — just let us know during the initial conversation.

Interior Design Services for Indian Homes

Whether you're moving into a new flat or finally redoing the home you've lived in for years, we tailor our involvement to what you actually need — from a single room to a full interior overhaul.

Nuvasah Interiors provides residential interior design services for homeowners across Delhi NCR, covering full home interiors, single-room makeovers, design consultation, and renovation planning for 1BHK to 4BHK homes.

Full Home Interiors

End-to-end design for 1BHK to 4BHK homes — space planning, modular kitchen and wardrobe design, furniture selection, lighting, and styling, all coordinated so nothing feels disjointed.

Single Room Makeovers

Living room, bedroom, or kitchen — sometimes one room is all that needs a rethink. We design focused solutions that work within a defined budget and timeline.

Design Consultation

Already have a contractor or working on it yourself? We offer concept design, material and color guidance, and layout planning — no full execution required, if that's all you need.

Renovation & Space Planning

For older homes that need smarter layouts — knocking down what doesn't work, opening up what does, and planning renovations that add real functionality.

How a Project Comes Together

Step 1

Understanding Your Space

We start with a conversation — your home, your family, how you use each room, and what's not working right now. If possible, a site visit or video walkthrough helps us understand the space beyond photos.

Step 2

Concept & Design

We put together a design concept — layout, material palette, and a sense of how the finished space will look and feel — built around your budget range and priorities, not ours.

Step 3

Refinement

Design is a back-and-forth. We revise based on your feedback until the plan feels right, before anything is finalized or ordered.

Step 4

Execution

Once the design is finalized, we manage execution — coordinating materials, craftsmanship, and on-ground work so you're not left chasing contractors on your own.

Step 5

Final Styling & Handover

The last details — decor, styling, finishing touches — that make a space feel complete, not just "done."
